# Using the website

A tour of every page, and how to find older stories.

## The top bar

| Link | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Latest** | Every news story, newest first (the front page). Obituaries are not here; they have their own Obituaries page. |
| **Local, Military, Business, Labor, Tourism, Sports, Food, Saipan & CNMI** | Only stories in that topic. A story can be in more than one topic. |
| **Obituaries** | Obituaries, death notices, rosaries and funerals from the news sites. They appear only here, not under Latest, the other topics or the archive. Search still finds them, so you can look up a name. This page can show an ad (see [Advertising](settings.md#advertising)). |
| **Archive** | Every story collected, organized by year, month and day |
| **Jobs** | Openings on Guam from local job boards (see [The Jobs page](#the-jobs-page)) |
| **Events** | What's coming up on Guam: sessions, hearings, public meetings and more (see [The Events page](#the-events-page)) |
| **Bids** | Open Government of Guam bids and requests for proposals (see [The Bids page](#the-bids-page)) |
| **Weather** | The weather, the sea and the tides on Guam (see [The Weather page](#the-weather-page)) |
| **Outages** | Power and water outages, and boil-water notices (see [The Outages page](#the-outages-page)) |
| **Search box** | Finds stories from any date (see [Searching](#searching)) |

The topic links come from the topic list; if you add a topic, it appears here
automatically (see [Topics](topics.md)). The **Sources** page (the news sites,
and whether each one is working; see [below](#the-sources-page)) is linked at
the bottom of every page, beside About this site and Disclaimer, to leave room
in the top bar.

## Weather warnings and the weather box

- **The banner** at the top of every page appears while the National Weather
  Service has a watch or a warning for Guam, for example a Typhoon Warning. A
  warning is red and a watch is amber. Click it to read the full text from
  the National Weather Service. The banner goes away when the warning ends.
- **The weather box** at the top of the home page shows the latest reading at
  Guam International Airport (temperature, how hot it feels, humidity, wind),
  the forecast (one line per period: the next four, or three on a tablet or a
  laptop with the sidebar, or two on a phone), the tropical storms and
  typhoons in the western North Pacific from the Joint Typhoon Warning Center
  (JTWC), and the advisories, such as a Small Craft Advisory. Click an
  advisory to read it and see when it ends. Point at a forecast to see the
  full forecast text. It also lists today's high and low tides, and links to
  the Weather page ("Forecast and tides").

## The Weather page

Everything about the weather, the sea and the tides on one page, with a row
of links at the top to jump to each part. Each part ends with where its data
comes from, with a link to that service.

- **Right now**: the reading at Guam International Airport (temperature, how
  hot it feels, humidity, wind), the ocean temperature, and sunrise, sunset
  and the moon's phase.
- **Watches, warnings and advisories**: every National Weather Service alert
  for Guam and its coastal waters. Click one to read it.
- **Next 24 hours**: the temperature and the chance of rain every two hours.
- **Tides**: the time of the next high or low tide, then three views to
  switch between: **Day** (a chart of the water's height through today, and
  today's tides), **Week** (the next 7 days) and **Month** (the next 30 days).
  Tides that have gone by are grey.
- **This week**: each day's high and low, the sky and the chance of rain.
  "Read the forecast in words" opens the full forecast text.
- **On the water**: the rip current risk and the surf height on each side of
  the island, from the surf forecast, and the wind and seas for boating, from
  the coastal waters forecast.
- **Typhoons and tropical storms**: the storms that the Joint Typhoon Warning
  Center has warnings for in the western North Pacific.
- **Be ready**: links to Guam Homeland Security (typhoon readiness levels),
  the weather radar, the satellite view, and more.

See [Weather](settings.md#weather) for where the weather comes from.

## The Outages page

The power and water outages that the island's utilities announce, and
boil-water notices, on one page. Outages are only here: they are not in the
warning banner or on the home page. All times are Chamorro Standard Time.

- **Boil-water notices**: the notices of the last 30 days from the Guam
  Waterworks Authority (GWA) and Guam EPA. A notice that is still in force
  has a red frame; one that has been lifted says **Lifted**.
- **Power (GPA)**: the Guam Power Authority's outage notices (planned work
  and emergencies), from GuamALERTS. Each shows the villages, the day and
  hours, the areas, and links to GPA's notice and its PDF (often a map).
- **Water (GWA)**: GWA's outage updates: emergency, scheduled and extended
  outages, with the villages, the day, the start and end times and the
  areas, linked to GWA's notice.

Power and water are each sorted into **Today** and **Coming up**, with the
last 3 days under **Earlier** (click to open). Each part ends with where its
notices come from, a phone number to report an outage, and when the site last
checked. If the site could not reach an agency for more than 6 hours, it says
so and links to that agency's page for the latest.

See [Outages and gas prices](settings.md#outages-and-gas-prices) for where the
notices come from.

## A story card

Each story shows:

- **Topic labels** (e.g. MILITARY). Click one to see all stories in that
  topic.
- **The headline.** Click it to open the full story on the news site, in a
  new tab.
- **The photo**, shown whole. Click it to open the story too.
- **The first paragraph or two** of the story.
- **The news site's name**, which opens that site's home page; then how long
  ago the story was published and, when known, who wrote it.
- **"Read the full story at …"**, which also opens the story.

Stories are grouped by day ("Today", "Yesterday", then dates), with the
newest story at the top. A story's time is the time the news site gives. If
the site gives no time, the story gets the time it was collected (for a story
that shows only a date, this applies when it is collected on that date). All
times are **Chamorro Standard Time (ChST)**.

## The sidebar

- **Updated** shows when the news was last checked. If this says more than
  about 1 hour 15 minutes ago, something may be wrong; see
  [Looking after it](maintenance.md#is-it-healthy).
- **Gas prices** shows the price of a gallon of regular, premium and diesel
  on Guam (the same at every station), with the day of the news story each
  price comes from and a link to it. It appears only when a news story from
  the last 45 days gave a price (see
  [Outages and gas prices](settings.md#outages-and-gas-prices)).
- **This week** shows how many stories each topic got in the last 7 days.
- **Sources** lists the news sites with this week's story counts. Click a
  **name** to see only that site's stories on your site. Click the **web
  address** under it (e.g. `guampdn.com ↗`) to open the news site itself.

## Searching

Type in the search box at the top and press **Enter**. Search looks at
headlines, first paragraphs, bylines and the news sites' own tags, for every
story ever collected.

| You type | It finds |
|---|---|
| `buildup contract` | stories with both words, in any form (*contracts*, *contracting*) |
| `"minimum wage"` | that exact phrase (use straight double quotes) |
| `typhoon or storm` | stories with either word |
| `buildup -Okinawa` | *buildup*, but not stories that mention *Okinawa* |
| `Hagatna` | *Hagåtña* too; accents don't matter |

Matching words are **highlighted** in the results.

If nothing matches exactly, it shows **headlines that look similar**, which
helps with typos (for example `Andersn` still finds *Andersen*).

### Narrowing a search

The search page has more options:

- **Source:** only one news site.
- **Topic:** only one topic, such as Local, Military or Obituaries.
- **From / To:** only stories published between two dates.
- **Sort:**
  - *Best match* puts the most relevant first, slightly favoring recent
    stories.
  - *Newest first* and *Oldest first* sort by date.

**Tip:** leave the words box empty and pick dates to see everything from that
period, for example everything the Daily Post published in March 2025.

Click **Clear** to reset all options.

## The archive

**Archive** lists every month that has stories, with a count. Click a month
to see its days, then a day to see that day's stories. **←** and **→** step
to the previous or next month (or day).

## The Jobs page

The **Jobs** link lists open positions on Guam, newest first, from the job
boards in `config/jobs.yaml`. Each posting shows its title (click to open it
on the board), who is hiring, pay, location, open and closing dates, the
start of the description, a link to the announcement PDF when there is one,
and **Scraped**: the last time it was found on its board. A dash means the
posting doesn't say. Use **Words** and **Board** to narrow the list. The
sidebar lists each board with its number of open postings, a red **⚠** when
its last check failed, and the boards that can't be collected. More in
[Jobs](jobs.md).

## The Events page

The **Events** link lists what's coming up on Guam over the next 90 days,
from the calendars in `config/events.yaml`, grouped by day (Today, Tomorrow,
then each date). The time of each event is on its left, in Guam time; an
event that started on an earlier day and is still going shows **Ongoing**.
Each event shows its name (click it for the event's own page, with buttons
that add it to Google Calendar or another calendar app), who holds it, when
and where it is, the start of its description, a link to its calendar, and
for Legislature sessions a link to the agenda. Use **Words** and **Calendar**
to narrow the list. Under the month calendar, **Google Calendar** and **Apple
Calendar or Outlook** subscribe to the events in a calendar app, which keeps
them up to date by itself (see [Adding the events to a calendar app](events.md#adding-the-events-to-a-calendar-app)). The sidebar lists each calendar with its number of upcoming events, a
red **⚠** when its last check failed, and the calendars that can't be
collected. More in [Events](events.md).

## The Bids page

The **Bids** link lists the Government of Guam's open bids and requests for
proposals (RFPs), from the lists in `config/bids.yaml`, soonest due first.
Each bid shows its kind and number, what it is for (click to open it at the
agency or portal that posted it), the agency, when offers are due in Guam
time with the days left, the day it was posted, the start of its text and a
link to the notice (PDF) when there is one. A bid posted in two places is
shown once, with "also on" and a link to the other listing. Bids leave the
page once their due date has passed. Use **Words**, **Agency** and
**Source** to narrow the list. The sidebar lists each source with its number
of open bids and a red **⚠** when its last check failed. More in
[Bids and RFPs](bids.md).

## Classifieds, the business directory and featured listings

These parts show once PayPal and `STATS_PASSWORD` are set (see
[Paid listings](settings.md#paid-listings)). The **Buy, sell and find local**
box in the sidebar and the links at the bottom of every page open them.

- **Classifieds** lists ads that people on Guam paid for: for sale, cars and
  trucks, housing, services and wanted. Each ad shows its section, title,
  price, village, a phone number or email for buyers, a few lines and a photo
  when there is one. Use **Words** and **Section** to narrow the list.
  **Post an ad** opens the form.
- **Business directory** lists Guam businesses by category and village.
  Featured entries come first, with a photo and a link to the business's
  website; the others follow by name. **Add your business** opens the form.
- **Featured job posts** are pinned at the top of the Jobs page, with a gold
  **Featured** tag. **Post a job** opens the form.
- **Featured events** are at the top of the Events page and on the home page,
  in gold on the month calendar, and in the calendar subscription, until the
  event is over. **Feature your event** opens the form.

After the form, the poster sees a private page with the listing and a **Pay
with PayPal** link (with a PayPal account or a card). The address of that
page is the poster's way back: there are no accounts. Each listing waits for
the site's owner to approve it on `/admin/listings` before it shows; a
rejected listing's payment is given back.

A classified ad, a featured job post and a business's featured upgrade end
by themselves after their number of days. A few days before, the poster gets
one email with a link to that private page, where **Renew** pays the same
price again through PayPal for as many days more (see
[Renewal reminders](settings.md#renewal-reminders)). A business that booked
an ad on the Advertise page gets the same kind of email, and its booking's
page has a **Run this ad again** form.

## The Sources page

The **Sources** link at the bottom of every page opens a page that starts with **Why this site
exists**: a short note on what the site is for. Below it is a table with one
row per news site:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source** | The site's name (click for its stories) and web address (click to visit it). A red **⚠** line is the last problem it had. **paused** means the site is switched off. |
| **Last check** | When GU Headlines last looked at this site. A red **failed** means that check didn't work at all. |
| **24 h / 7 days** | New stories saved in the last day and week |
| **Total** | All stories ever saved from this site |
| **Latest story** | When its newest story was published |

Below the table, **Can't be collected** lists the news sites that turn away
automated visitors, with the reason and a link to visit each one directly.
GU Headlines doesn't try to read them. The sidebar lists them too, under the
other sources.

The **Disclaimer** says that the site writes none of the stories, that the
headlines, excerpts and photos belong to their publishers, and that the only
aim is to send readers to them. The links at the bottom of every page lead to
these two parts.

**Recent runs** lists the latest checks. For each check it shows how many
links were found (**Links**), how many new stories were saved (**New**), and
any problem.

What the red messages mean, and what to do about them, is in
[News sources](news-sources.md#reading-the-sources-page).

## Light and dark

The switch at the right of the top bar (a sun in light mode, a moon in dark
mode) changes between a paper-colored page and a dark one. Both have a
flat, matte look, like the screen of a color e-reader.

- On a first visit, the site follows your device's light or dark setting.
  Devices without a setting count as light; browsers report them that way.
- If the browser gives no answer at all (some older browsers, or JavaScript
  switched off), the site is dark.
- Your choice is remembered in that browser. Switch back to the mode your
  device uses, and the site follows your device again.

It works on phones too: the menu scrolls sideways, and photos appear above
each story.
