# Using the website

A tour of every page, and how to find older stories.

## The top bar

| Link | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Latest** | Every news story, newest first (the front page). r/guam posts are not here; they have their own Community page. |
| **Local, Military, Business, Labor** | Only stories in that topic. A story can be in more than one topic. |
| **Community** | Posts from Reddit's r/guam: questions, tips and discussion from people on island. They appear only here, and in search when you choose the Community topic. |
| **Archive** | Every story collected, organized by year, month and day |
| **Sources** | The news sites, and whether each one is working (see [below](#the-sources-page)) |
| **Search box** | Finds stories from any date (see [Searching](#searching)) |

The topic links come from the topic list; if you add a topic, it appears here
automatically (see [Topics](topics.md)).

## A story card

Each story shows:

- **Topic labels** (e.g. MILITARY). Click one to see all stories in that
  topic.
- **The headline.** Click it to open the full story on the news site, in a
  new tab.
- **The photo**, shown whole. Click it to open the story too.
- **The first paragraph or two** of the story.
- **The news site's name**, which opens that site's home page; then how long
  ago the story was published and, when known, who wrote it.
- **"Read the full story at …"**, which also opens the story.

Stories are grouped by day ("Today", "Yesterday", then dates). All times are
**Chamorro Standard Time (ChST)**.

## The sidebar

- **Updated** shows when the news was last checked. If this says more than
  about 1 hour 15 minutes ago, something may be wrong; see
  [Looking after it](maintenance.md#is-it-healthy).
- **This week** shows how many stories each topic got in the last 7 days.
- **Sources** lists the news sites with this week's story counts. Click a
  **name** to see only that site's stories on your site. Click the **web
  address** under it (e.g. `guampdn.com ↗`) to open the news site itself.

## Searching

Type in the search box at the top and press **Enter**. Search looks at
headlines, first paragraphs, bylines and the news sites' own tags, for every
story ever collected.

| You type | It finds |
|---|---|
| `buildup contract` | stories with both words, in any form (*contracts*, *contracting*) |
| `"minimum wage"` | that exact phrase (use straight double quotes) |
| `typhoon or storm` | stories with either word |
| `buildup -Okinawa` | *buildup*, but not stories that mention *Okinawa* |
| `Hagatna` | *Hagåtña* too; accents don't matter |

Matching words are **highlighted** in the results.

If nothing matches exactly, it shows **headlines that look similar**, which
helps with typos (for example `Andersn` still finds *Andersen*).

### Narrowing a search

The search page has more options:

- **Source:** only one news site.
- **Topic:** only Local, Military, Business, Labor or Community.
- **From / To:** only stories published between two dates.
- **Sort:**
  - *Best match* puts the most relevant first, slightly favoring recent
    stories.
  - *Newest first* and *Oldest first* sort by date.

**Tip:** leave the words box empty and pick dates to see everything from that
period, for example everything the Daily Post published in March 2025.

Click **Clear** to reset all options.

## The archive

**Archive** lists every month that has stories, with a count. Click a month
to see its days, then a day to see that day's stories. **←** and **→** step
to the previous or next month (or day).

## The Sources page

The **Sources** link at the top opens a table with one row per news site:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source** | The site's name (click for its stories) and web address (click to visit it). A red **⚠** line is the last problem it had. **paused** means the site is switched off. |
| **Last check** | When GU Headlines last looked at this site. A red **failed** means that check didn't work at all. |
| **24 h / 7 days** | New stories saved in the last day and week |
| **Total** | All stories ever saved from this site |
| **Latest story** | When its newest story was published |

Below the table, **Can't be collected** lists the news sites that turn away
automated visitors, with the reason and a link to visit each one directly.
GU Headlines doesn't try to read them. The sidebar lists them too, under the
other sources.

**Recent runs** lists the latest checks. For each check it shows how many
links were found (**Links**), how many new stories were saved (**New**), and
any problem.

What the red messages mean, and what to do about them, is in
[News sources](news-sources.md#reading-the-sources-page).

## Light and dark

The switch at the right of the top bar (a sun in light mode, a moon in dark
mode) changes between a white page and a dark one.

- On a first visit, the site follows your device's light or dark setting.
  Devices without a setting count as light; browsers report them that way.
- If the browser gives no answer at all (some older browsers, or JavaScript
  switched off), the site is dark.
- Your choice is remembered in that browser. Switch back to the mode your
  device uses, and the site follows your device again.

It works on phones too: the menu scrolls sideways, and photos appear above
each story.
