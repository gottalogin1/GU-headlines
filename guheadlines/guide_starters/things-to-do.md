Guam is about 30 miles long and between 4 and 12 miles wide, so nothing is far away. You can stay in Tumon and still reach a wildlife refuge, a Spanish fort, a war memorial and a jungle waterfall in a day. This list sorts the island's best things to do by what you enjoy, with a guide for each.

## Beaches and the sea

- **Swim and snorkel in Tumon Bay,** a calm lagoon right in front of the hotels, then watch the sun set into the sea. See [Tumon and Two Lovers Point](/guides/tumon).
- **Snorkel or dive** the Piti Bomb Holes, a famous pair of shipwrecks or the reef at Gun Beach. See [Snorkeling and diving](/guides/snorkeling-and-diving).
- **Go out on a boat** to look for spinner dolphins, take a sunset cruise or spend a day on Cocos Island. See [Boats, dolphins and island trips](/guides/on-the-water).
- **Learn how the reef works** before you swim. See [Beaches and staying safe in the water](/guides/beaches-and-water-safety).

## History

- **Walk the beaches where US forces landed in 1944,** and climb to Japanese guns that still stand in the hills. See [World War II sites](/guides/war-history).
- **See the latte stones and ancient village sites** of the CHamoru people. See [Latte stones and ancient CHamoru sites](/guides/ancient-chamorro-sites).
- **Spend a morning in Hagåtña,** the capital, with its Spanish plaza, cathedral and the Guam Museum. See [A morning in Hagåtña](/guides/hagatna-walking).

## The outdoors

- **Hike to the top of Mount Lamlam,** Guam's highest point, or to a jungle waterfall. See [Hiking and waterfalls](/guides/hiking-and-waterfalls).
- **Drive the southern villages,** with mountain lookouts, quiet bays and a Spanish fort. See [Driving the southern villages](/guides/south-loop-drive).
- **Explore the north,** from the cliffs of Two Lovers Point to the white sand of Ritidian. See [A day in northern Guam](/guides/northern-guam).

## Food, markets and culture

- **Eat your way through a night market,** or get up early for the weekend flea market. See [Markets and shopping](/guides/markets-and-shopping).
- **Try red rice, kelaguen and island barbecue.** See [Local food to try](/guides/local-food).
- **Join a village fiesta** or time your trip for Liberation Day. See [Fiestas and festivals through the year](/guides/festivals-and-fiestas).
- **Learn a few words of CHamoru** and the island's customs. See [Local customs and being a good guest](/guides/culture-and-respect).

## Families and rainy days

## A few days on Guam

If you have four or five days, here is one way to see the island:

1. **Day one:** settle in, swim in Tumon Bay and watch the sunset from the beach.
2. **Day two:** Hagåtña in the morning, then the war sites at Asan and Piti. If it is a Wednesday, end at the night market.
3. **Day three:** drive the southern villages, with a swim at the Inarajan pools if the sea is calm.
4. **Day four:** northern Guam, with Ritidian and Two Lovers Point.
5. **Day five:** a snorkel, a dive or a boat trip, and some shopping.

> Before a beach day, a boat trip or a hike, check the surf, the warnings and the forecast on the [weather page](/weather). The [events calendar](/events) shows what is on while you are here.
