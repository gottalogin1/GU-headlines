About these awards

When an agency picks the company that gets a contract, the bid is awarded. These lists don't give the day of the award itself, so each award is dated by when its bids were due; the award usually follows within weeks.

The amount is what the agency lists. For supplies bought as needed, it can be a yearly or not-to-exceed figure. The bid abstract, where linked, shows every company's price.

Only some agencies publish who won. Always check the details with the agency.
