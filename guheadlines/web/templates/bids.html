About these listings

An invitation for bid asks companies for prices on something the agency has described exactly; the lowest qualified bid wins. A request for proposals describes a need and picks the best proposal, not only the cheapest.

Due dates and times are Guam time (ChST). When a notice gives no due date here, it is in the agency's documents; such a bid stays listed for 30 days after it was posted. A bid posted in two places is shown once.

Bid packets, registration and any changes (amendments) come from the agency. Always check the details with the agency before you bid.
