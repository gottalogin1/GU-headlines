How it works

  • The site reads the same bid lists as the Bids page every {{ settings.bids_interval_hours }} hours.
  • When a new bid matches your trades or your own words, you get one email with the new bids: the agency, the due date and a link to the notice.
  • No account or password. Every email has a link to your own page, where you can change your trades or words, or cancel.
  • You pay on PayPal's own page, with a PayPal account or a card. PayPal takes {{ alerts.price }} each month until you cancel. When you cancel, the alerts go on until the end of the month you paid for.
