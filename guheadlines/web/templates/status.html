Why this site exists

Guam's news is spread across many outlets: newspapers, television and radio stations, government agencies, military commands and community groups. {{ site }} brings their headlines together in one place, so that anyone who follows the island can see at a glance what is being reported and choose what to read.

Each story appears with its headline, a short excerpt and its photo, and links to the full article on the publisher's own website. When a headline catches your interest, you read the story where it was published. The outlets listed below do the reporting. This site exists to help readers find their work.

Search engines such as Google collect and use more than that. They read the full text of every article they index, and their results often show a headline, a snippet and a photo as well. {{ site }} keeps only the headline, a short excerpt and the photo, and never stores the rest of the story.
