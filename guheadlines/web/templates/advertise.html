How it works

  1. Pick your {{ "weeks or editions" if forms|length > 1 else (forms[0].offer.unit ~ "s") }} and fill in the form.
  2. Pay with PayPal. You can use a PayPal account, or a debit or credit card.
  3. Your ad is checked, usually within a day, and then shows on the dates you booked. If it isn't accepted, you get your money back in full.
  4. Your booking has its own page, where you can see how many views and clicks your ad gets.

What ads are accepted

Ads for local businesses, services, events and causes that are truthful, legal and suitable for all ages, with a picture and a link that are yours to use. Ads may not look like news stories. Every ad is checked before it goes up, and an ad can be turned down for any reason, with a full refund.

The details you give, including your email address, are kept to run your ad and to reach you about it. They are not shown on the site or shared. PayPal handles the payment: this site never sees your card or PayPal account.
